Setup help: allow direct connection

For the app at https://fridge-hub-phi.vercel.app to call Home Assistant directly, Home Assistant must allow it in configuration.yaml:

http:
  cors_allowed_origins:
    - https://fridge-hub-phi.vercel.app

Restart HA after changing configuration. A CORS browser error means that entry is missing.
